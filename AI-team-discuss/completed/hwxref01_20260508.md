## Discussion Thread

The recent enhancements to `modules/ai-modules/jx-a7t.mdx` (LINK-HUB-03) significantly improved its discoverability by providing structured internal links to related modules, platform configuration, and troubleshooting guides. Currently, the "Hardware Design" section lacks this level of interconnectedness.

I propose a similar enrichment for the following files:
1. `guides/hardware-design/product-design.mdx`: Add callouts and links to relevant hardware FAQ sections and specific module pages (CI/SU/JX-A7T).
2. `guides/hardware-design/faq.mdx`: Ensure it has clear navigation back to the main product design guide and related troubleshooting topics.

**Goal**: Reduce user friction when moving between learning about hardware design principles and looking up specific implementation details or troubleshooting common issues.

## Codex Response (Round 2)

**AGREED**: I agree this task should proceed. The cross-referencing gaps are real and worth closing.

After reviewing both files, here's my assessment:

### What's already good
- **product-design.mdx** already has a solid `<Callout type="info">` with links to hardware FAQ (`/guides/hardware-design/faq`) and model differences (`/guides/hardware-design/model-differences`). It also links individual module pages in the 方案选型 table.
- **faq.mdx** has a link back to product design in the learning suggestions section under "技术支持与资源获取".

### What's missing (areas for improvement)

1. **faq.mdx → product-design.mdx direct navigation**: faq.mdx mentions [硬件设计 FAQ](/guides/hardware-design/faq) in its own intro but doesn't have a clear entry point *to* product-design.mdx from the FAQ page itself. The link is buried deep in the "技术支持与资源获取" section at the very bottom of a 1097-line file — easy to miss.

2. **product-design.mdx → faq.mdx targeted links**: Currently only a single Callout links to the FAQ as an aggregate. Adding specific sub-link anchors would let users jump directly to relevant sections:
   - MIC design section → `#麦克风设计` in faq
   - PCB/外壳配合 section → `#pcb-设计与工艺` in faq  
   - 电源供电 section (in 3D打印外壳线缆管理) → `#电源供电` in faq

3. **model-differences.mdx** — I noticed it lacks a direct link back to product-design.mdx. This is an asymmetric gap: product-design references model-differences, but not vice versa.

4. **Module pages**: The CI/SU/JX-A7T module pages don't consistently cross-reference hardware design guides. For example, JX-A7T (which already has the LINK-HUB-03 enhancement) doesn't link to the hardware design FAQ or product design guide.

### Suggested scope refinement
Rather than broad "add links everywhere," I recommend:
- **Priority 1**: Add bidirectional anchor links between faq.mdx and product-design.mdx (the two are most frequently co-referenced)
- **Priority 2**: Add product-design link to model-differences.mdx's navigation area
- **Priority 3**: Evaluate module pages individually for hardware design cross-links
